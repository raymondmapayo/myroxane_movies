! Loaded into Brave's adblock-rust engine (see lib/core/config/ad_blocking.dart).
! Network rules target known ad/tracking/popunder domains seen on the
! streaming mirror sites embedded in the player. Cosmetic rules hide leftover
! ad containers that the network layer alone won't remove.

! Two tiers of network rule live below. Third-party ad/popunder/tracking
! domains are safe to block on sight: no mirror serves video from them, so a
! wrong entry costs nothing. First-party rules naming a mirror's own host are
! the opposite — they must come from watching that host's traffic, because a
! path guessed wrong either blocks nothing or takes the stream down with it.
